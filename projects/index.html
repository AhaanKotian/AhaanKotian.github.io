Deep Learning

Generative Deep Learning for Drug Discovery

Generated novel chemical compounds for drug discovery targeting inhibition of Alzheimer’s disease progression. Sourced chemical data from ChemBL in SMILES syntax, designed a custom tokeniser, and used an improved LSTM model to achieve 83.50% validation accuracy, generating 4 valid molecules verified with RDKit.

Vision-Language Models

Visual Question Answering for Autonomous Driving

Implemented a Visual Question Answering (VQA) pipeline to evaluate the scene understanding capabilities of diverse Vision-Language Models (VLMs) in autonomous driving contexts. Curated and pre-processed the DriveLM dataset, and fine-tuned SmolVLM and Qwen2.5-VL-3B-Instruct using LoRA and QLoRA, attaining exact-match accuracy of 56.68% and 65.78% respectively on unseen test data.

Jan 1, 2025
Multi-Robot Systems

Artificial Potential Field Planner for Multi-Robot Simulation

Simulated multi-robot coordination in Gazebo using an Artificial Potential Field (APF) planner to achieve arbitrary formations around a target pose. Architected the simulation framework and optimized URDF models for efficient execution of multiple Volta robots in low-resource environments.
