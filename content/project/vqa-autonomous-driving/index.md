title: Visual Question Answering for Autonomous Driving
---

Implemented a Visual Question Answering (VQA) pipeline to evaluate the scene understanding capabilities of diverse Vision-Language Models (VLMs) in autonomous driving contexts. Curated and pre-processed the DriveLM dataset, and fine-tuned SmolVLM and Qwen2.5-VL-3B-Instruct using LoRA and QLoRA, attaining exact-match accuracy of 56.68% and 65.78% respectively on unseen test data.
