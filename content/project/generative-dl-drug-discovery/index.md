title: Generative Deep Learning for Drug Discovery

Generated novel chemical compounds for drug discovery targeting inhibition of Alzheimer's disease progression. Sourced chemical data from ChemBL in SMILES syntax, designed a custom tokeniser, and used an improved LSTM model to achieve 83.50% validation accuracy, generating 4 valid molecules verified with RDKit.
